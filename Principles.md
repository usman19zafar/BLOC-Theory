PRINCIPLES (The Operational Rules of BLOC Theory)


Principle 1 — The Binary Tunnel Defines the Ceiling
All digital cognition passes through:
World → Encoding → Binary Processing → Decoding → Output  
This tunnel sets the upper limit of machine cognition.



Principle 2 — Representation ≠ Reality
Machines operate on representations of the world,
not the world itself.



Principle 3 — Meaning Is Lost at the Point of Encoding
The moment a phenomenon becomes 0/1,
its phenomenological content is stripped away.



Principle 4 — Machines Can Simulate Behavior, Not Experience
Robots can imitate human actions,
but cannot generate human meaning.



Principle 5 — Alignment Is the Only Achievable Goal
Since imitation of human cognition is impossible,
the correct engineering target is cognitive alignment,
not replication.

Principle 6 — Organic Outcomes Require Organic Processing
Human‑like decisions require human‑like cognition.
Binary systems cannot produce truly organic outcomes.
