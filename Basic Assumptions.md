BASIC ASSUMPTIONS (BLOC Theory Core)


Assumption 1 — Binary is a Transform, Not a Mirror
Any phenomenon converted into binary is altered, not preserved.
Encoding is not neutral; it is a filter.


Assumption 2 — Phenomenology Cannot Survive Discretization
Human experience is continuous, embodied, and analog.
Binary systems cannot carry this richness through the digital pipeline.


Assumption 3 — Computation Cannot Produce Experience on Its Current Path
As long as computing remains binary, symbolic, and representational,
it cannot generate organic cognition.


Assumption 4 — Human and Machine Cognition Are Ontologically Different
Humans experience signals.
Machines process signals.
These are different categories of existence.


Assumption 5 — Scaling Compute Does Not Close the Gap
More data, more layers, more power cannot restore what binary encoding removes.
